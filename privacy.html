Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026

MKC+ is not watching you

Most apps today collect what you do and send it somewhere. MKC+ was built the other way around. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no history, and it does not know who you are. What you type, copy, move or drop never leaves your local network.

Your devices talk only to each other

Your mouse, keyboard, clipboard and files go straight from one of your devices to another over your own network, encrypted from end to end. No server sits in the middle.

What goes online, and only this

  • Feedback, if you choose to send it. Nothing else in the Google Play app ever reaches the internet.
  • One update check a day, in MKC+ for Windows, macOS and Linux and the Android app from mkcplus.com (Google Play updates its own copy). It goes through a counter on Cloudflare that adds one to that day's total for the system and for what Cloudflare reads from the connection: country and region, network name and local hour. It keeps no IP address and no device identifier, so it counts installs without knowing whose they are. Network and region totals are deleted after 90 days. A Linux machine using MKC+'s dnf repository also fetches each new version through the counter, which adds one for the version, the system dnf names and the country.

When you send feedback

Feedback is the one time MKC+ sends anything about you, and only what you choose: your message, and if you add them, your name, email address, screenshots, pictures, and a diagnostics bundle (system details, settings, network addresses, device identifiers and recent logs). It goes over an encrypted connection to our relay on Cloudflare and is saved as a private item on GitHub that only the developer can read. Screenshots and pictures are deleted after 30 days. The relay holds your IP address and device identifier for at most 20 minutes, only to stop anyone from flooding it.

Android permissions

Accessibility lets the mouse and keyboard of your other devices control your phone. MKC+ reads only what is under the pointer, to click and scroll, never the rest of your screen. It reads the clipboard only when the pointer leaves for another device, so you can paste there. It takes a picture of the screen once, to show your wallpaper on your other devices, and once more only if you add a screenshot to feedback. While your mouse is on another device, it sends the keys of a keyboard connected to your phone there. None of this leaves your own devices unless you send it as feedback.

Deleting your data

The only data we can hold is feedback you sent. To have it deleted, email support@mkcplus.com from the address you used, or with the date you sent it, and we delete it within 30 days.

Children

MKC+ is not directed at children under 13.

This website

mkcplus.com uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not follow you to other sites. When you press a download button, the page tells the same counter which button and the page's language, and it adds one to that day's total for those and your country. It keeps no IP address and no identifier here either.

Contact and changes

support@mkcplus.com. If MKC+ ever changes what it sends, this page will say so first.
